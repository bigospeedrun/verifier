Reading: the paper uses s0 = 1 - 1/1300. The moment inequality first holds at N* = 1299,
so 1300 is a rank count taken just above its own threshold -- which is why the
constant 1301/1302 is computed rather than chosen.

Two statements must be kept apart, and both were checked exactly:

  (a) at the paper's own s0 = 1-1/1300 the chain gives kappa = 7.6834387775e-4, which is
      LESS than 1/1301 = 7.6863950807e-4 -- so a claim of kappa = 1/1301 would be false
      AS WRITTEN, and 1301/1302 is the tight integer rung at that parameter;

  (b) at the true threshold s0 = 1-1/1299 the SAME chain gives
      kappa = 29646335589512201/38555076544413512201 = 7.6893468375e-4 ~ 1/1300.5, which
      is GREATER than 1/1301 -- so a stronger kappa = 1/1301 row is supportable by
      changing one digit, using the certificate already shipped.

Net: the published row is correct and conservative by about one unit of the
denominator. It understates, it does not inflate. No row change is recommended on
the strength of this note.
